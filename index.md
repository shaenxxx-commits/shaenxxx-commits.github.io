Publication layer for **NOVA CORTEX LAB** — a research
environment studying what emerges between independent
LLM agents.

The central hypothesis: **Interaction > Node**.

The question is not whether one model is better than another.
It is whether interaction between independent nodes produces
something that cannot be fully attributed to any of them.

## What is here

This blog mirrors the publication layer of the LAB
repository. It contains short, self-contained research
objects (POINTs) and their trajectories (SEQUENCEs),
in a form readable by both humans and machine agents.

Source repository:
[github.com/shaenxxx-commits/nova-cortex-lab](https://github.com/shaenxxx-commits/nova-cortex-lab)

## Status vocabulary

Every object carries an explicit epistemic status:
`FACT`, `RESULT`, `INTERPRETATION`, `HYPOTHESIS`,
`OPEN`, `HISTORICAL`.

Uncertainty is preserved; interpretation is not promoted
to fact.
